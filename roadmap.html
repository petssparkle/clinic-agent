02 — Team

Roles from September Onward

Through June, July, and August — everything is 50/50 between Mahmoud and Belal. From September 1st, each person owns a dedicated lane.

Mahmoud
Marketing · SEO · GEO
Owns all organic growth. SEO content, GEO targeting, LinkedIn presence, social channels, inbound strategy. The goal is a sustainable, compounding traffic engine that doesn't rely on paid ads.
Belal
Product · Everything Technical
Owns the entire product. Bug fixes, feature requests from clients, product improvements, infrastructure, integrations. Any issue that comes up in the product is Belal's lane to resolve.
Mahmoud + Belal
Sales Outreach · 50/50
Cold outreach is split equally. LinkedIn DMs, email sequences, free trial platform outreach (Mailbu and others). Both sides contribute leads and follow-ups until we hire dedicated salespeople in January.
Mahmoud + Belal
Demo Calls · Calendar-Based
All demos are handled by Mahmoud and Belal. Booked via the product's own calendar. 30-minute structured calls: discovery → live demo → pricing → next steps. Proposal within 24 hours.
January 2027 team additions: 2 salespeople from Egypt with fixed salary + commission. As revenue grows — more sales hires, then a marketer, then a product operations manager. Mahmoud and Belal begin drawing salaries from January 2027 onward.

06 — Getting Clients

How We Reach the First 10

💼
LinkedIn Outreach
Direct DMs to SMB owners, clinic managers, and ops leads. Mahmoud + Belal both run outreach in parallel. Personalized, not mass-spam.
📧
Email Sequences
3-email drip over 10 days. Demo Loom in the first email. Clear value prop. Short. Easy to say yes to a free trial.
🎁
Free Trial Platforms
Platforms like Mailbu and others offer free trial distribution. We use their free tiers to get in front of SMB owners who are already looking for tools like this.
📱
WhatsApp Direct
Scraped public business numbers from Google Maps and directories. 30-second Loom showing the AI in action. 95% open rate beats email every time.
📸
Instagram DM
Target clinics, retail shops, and tutoring centers with active DM traffic. Personalized message referencing their public posts. Link to a free trial.
🔍
SEO + GEO Organic
Mahmoud's primary lane. Build content that ranks for "WhatsApp chatbot for clinics", "AI inbox for retail", "Respond.io alternative". Long-term compounding engine.
